MHA — NATIONAL CYBERCRIME REPORTING AND THE 1930 HELPLINE

Issuer: MHA

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1.  Reporting
Victims of financial cyber fraud can call the 1930 helpline or file a complaint on the national cybercrime reporting portal.  The complaint is shared with banks and payment providers through the citizen financial cyber fraud reporting system.

2. Golden hour
Funds can often be frozen if the complaint reaches the beneficiary bank before the fraudster moves the money onward. Reporting within the first hour gives the best chance of recovery.

3. Bank action
On receiving a complaint, banks should place a lien or hold on the amount in the beneficiary account, trace onward transfers and update the status on the system so that the trail can be followed across banks.
