NPCI — FRAUD RISK MONITORING IN UPI

Issuer: NPCI   

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus.  It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1. Central monitoring
NPCI provides fraud risk monitoring services that score UPI transactions using patterns seen across the whole network. Banks combine this score with their own rules before approving or declining a transaction.

2. Shared intelligence
Network-level monitoring can spot a mule account receiving payments from customers of many banks, which a single bank cannot see. Banks are expected to act on alerts and report confirmed fraud back to improve the models.

3.  Feedback loop
Investigator outcomes, such as confirmed fraud, false positive or customer error, should be recorded in a structured way so they can be fed back as labels for model training.
