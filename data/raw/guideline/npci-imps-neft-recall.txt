NPCI — RECALL OF FRAUDULENT IMPS AND ACCOUNT TRANSFERS

Issuer: NPCI   

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1. Recall
When a customer reports that an IMPS or account transfer was fraudulent, the remitter bank sends a recall request to the beneficiary bank. The beneficiary bank checks whether the funds are still in the account and places a hold if they are.

2. Return of funds
Funds held under a fraud complaint are usually returned only after the beneficiary account holder has had a chance to respond, or on the order of a court or law enforcement agency.   

3.  Investigator checks
Check how quickly the beneficiary moved the money, whether it went to other accounts at the same bank, and whether the beneficiary account has other fraud complaints.
