NPCI — DELEGATED UPI PAYMENTS

Issuer: NPCI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1. Delegation
A primary user can let a trusted secondary user make UPI payments from the primary user's account, either with full delegation inside a monthly limit or with approval for each payment.

2.  Risk
Delegation can be abused in coercion and family-fraud cases.  Investigators should check who set up the delegation, when, and from which device, before treating a secondary user’s payment as unauthorised.
