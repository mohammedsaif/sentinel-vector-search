NPCI — UPI COLLECT REQUESTS AND PULL-PAYMENT RISK

Issuer: NPCI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1.  How collect works
A collect request lets a payee ask a payer to approve a debit.  The payer sees the request in their app and must enter their UPI PIN to approve it.

2. Abuse
Fraudsters send collect requests with notes such as ’refund’, ’cashback’ or ’receive money’, hoping the victim thinks entering the PIN will receive funds. Entering the UPI PIN always sends money; it never receives it.

3. Controls
NPCI has moved to restrict person-to-person collect requests and limit their value. Apps should show clear warnings on collect requests and let customers block unknown requesters.
