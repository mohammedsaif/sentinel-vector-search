NPCI — UPI TRANSACTION LIMITS

Issuer: NPCI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1. Standard limit
The standard per-transaction UPI limit for person-to-person payments is one lakh rupees, and banks can set lower limits for their customers. Many banks also cap the number of transactions per day.

2. Higher category limits
Certain merchant categories such as tax payments, hospitals, educational institutions, capital markets and insurance can accept higher UPI amounts. Investigators should check the merchant category code before treating a high-value transaction as unusual.

3.  New user limits
Banks and apps often apply a reduced limit for the first day after a customer registers on a new device or app.  This cooling-off limit is a key control against account takeover.
