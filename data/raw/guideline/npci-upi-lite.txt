NPCI — UPI LITE AND SMALL-VALUE ON-DEVICE PAYMENTS

Issuer: NPCI   

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1.  Design
UPI Lite lets customers make small-value payments from an on-device balance without entering a UPI PIN for each payment.  The balance and per-payment limits are kept small to limit loss.

2. Risk
If a phone is stolen while unlocked, the Lite balance can be spent. Customers should be able to disable Lite and move the balance back to their account remotely.
