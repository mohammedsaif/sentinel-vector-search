RBI — CONTROLS ON NEW BENEFICIARIES AND FIRST TRANSACTIONS

Issuer: RBI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1.  Cooling period
Many banks apply a cooling period after a beneficiary is added, during which only a small amount can be sent.  This gives the genuine customer time to see the alert and stop an attacker.

2. Name check
Showing the beneficiary's registered name before the payment is confirmed helps customers spot when they are about to pay someone unexpected.

3.  Signals
Adding several beneficiaries in a short time, adding a beneficiary right after a password or device change, or sending the first transfer to a new beneficiary at night are all risk signals.
