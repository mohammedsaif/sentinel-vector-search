RBI — CARD TOKENISATION AND CARD-ON-FILE

Issuer: RBI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus.  It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1.  Tokenisation
Merchants and payment aggregators are not allowed to store actual card numbers.  Card details are replaced with tokens that are specific to the merchant and device. A stolen token is of limited use outside its context.

2. Customer consent
Tokenisation must be done only with explicit customer consent and additional factor authentication. Customers must be able to view and delete their tokens.

3. Fraud angle
Card-testing attacks and data breaches at merchants are less damaging when card-on-file data is tokenised. Investigators looking at card-not-present fraud should check whether the transaction used a token, and which token requestor created it.
