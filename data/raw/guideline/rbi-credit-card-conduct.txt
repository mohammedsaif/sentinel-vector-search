RBI — CREDIT CARD CONDUCT AND DISPUTE RULES

Issuer: RBI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus.  It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1.  Disputes
Card issuers must have a process for disputed transactions, give customers a provisional credit while the dispute is investigated where the rules require it, and keep customers informed of progress.

2. Unsolicited cards
Issuing or upgrading a card without explicit consent is prohibited. If a customer is billed for a card they did not ask for, the issuer must reverse the charges.   

3. Lost cards
Once a customer reports a lost or stolen card, they are not liable for transactions after the report. The issuer must block the card immediately and confirm the block.
