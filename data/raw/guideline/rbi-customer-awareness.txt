RBI — CUSTOMER AWARENESS ON DIGITAL FRAUD

Issuer: RBI   

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1. Messages
Customers should be told repeatedly that the bank will never ask for their PIN, OTP, password or CVV, never ask them to install screen-sharing apps, and never ask them to scan a QR code to receive money.

2. Digital arrest
A growing scam involves callers posing as police, customs or regulator officials who claim the victim is under investigation and must transfer money to a 'safe account'. No real agency arrests people over video calls or asks for money transfers.

3. Role of investigators
When a case involves social engineering, the investigator should record the script the fraudster used so that awareness messages can be updated.
