RBI — E-MANDATES FOR RECURRING CARD AND UPI PAYMENTS

Issuer: RBI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.

1. Registration
A recurring payment mandate on a card or UPI must be registered with additional factor authentication. The customer must get a pre-debit notification before each charge, with an option to opt out.

2.  Thresholds
Recurring debits up to a set value can run without additional authentication for each charge, while higher amounts need it.  Some categories such as insurance premiums and mutual fund subscriptions have a higher threshold.   

3. Disputes
Many chargebacks labelled as subscription fraud are cases where the customer forgot a mandate or a free trial converted to paid. Investigators should check the mandate registration record and pre-debit notification log before treating the case as unauthorised.
