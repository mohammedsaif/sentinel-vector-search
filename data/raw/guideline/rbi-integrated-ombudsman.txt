RBI — INTEGRATED OMBUDSMAN SCHEME AND INTERNAL GRIEVANCE REDRESS

Issuer: RBI   

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1.  Escalation
A customer can approach the ombudsman if their complaint to the regulated entity is rejected, is not satisfactorily resolved, or receives no reply within thirty days.  The scheme covers banks, non-bank payment system participants and many NBFCs.

2.  Internal ombudsman
Larger entities must appoint an internal ombudsman who reviews complaints that the entity proposes to reject in whole or in part.  This creates an independent check before a customer is told no.

3.  Implication for fraud teams
Every rejected fraud dispute may be reviewed later.  Case notes should therefore show the evidence used, the timeline of the customer's report and the reasoning behind the liability decision.
