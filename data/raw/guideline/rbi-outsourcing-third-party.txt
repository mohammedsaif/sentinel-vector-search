RBI — OUTSOURCING AND THIRD-PARTY RISK IN PAYMENTS

Issuer: RBI

Note: synthetic summary prepared for the SENTINEL coursework corpus. It paraphrases public guidance and is not an official RBI or NPCI text.   

1. Accountability
A regulated entity remains responsible for activities it outsources, including customer onboarding, collections and technology services. Contracts must give the entity audit rights and require prompt reporting of incidents.

2. Insider risk
Staff at outsourced call centres and business correspondents can misuse customer data. Controls include masking of sensitive fields, access logging, and periodic review of who looked at which account.
