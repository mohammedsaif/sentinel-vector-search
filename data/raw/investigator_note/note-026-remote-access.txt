Case SEN-2026-64596 | Typology suspected: Remote access app scam | Status: Customer error   

Summary
Customer called the helpline to report the transaction. Customer is based in Kochi. Disputed amount Rs 442,811 via mobile banking at 09:55. Session originated from Jamtara on Android device (Realme).   

Observations
Noted a remote access application was installed shortly before the transactions. Noted transactions made while the customer was on a call. Noted screen-sharing session active during the banking session. Noted unusual touch or typing patterns compared with the customer's behaviour.
